Against an independent solver

The reference (bench/ref.py) is written separately in Python. For rounds it bisects on the price and enumerates which safes take their cap. For exits it pays out every combination of conversion choices and checks that no class could do better by switching.

How it works

Your history is the input

List what happened, in order. Founders, pool, grants, safes, notes, priced rounds. Every screen is computed off that list, so tweak an early safe and every later round + every exit moves with it.

Post-money safes

A post-money safe converting at its cap owns amount ÷ cap of the company capitalization: every share, option and the existing pool, plus all converting safes and notes, but not the pool increase or the new money. Since the safes are inside that number it's solved together: capitalization = other shares ÷ (1 − Σ amount/cap). A safe with a discount takes whichever price is lower, cap or discounted round price. Each safe's pick changes the others, so it loops until nothing moves.

Pre-money safes + notes

Priced off the capitalization before any safe converts, pool increase included. Notes convert principal + simple interest.

The option pool shuffle

When the VC asks for "10% pool post-round", that increase is counted before their money comes in. So it dilutes you, not them. Round price = pre-money ÷ (company capitalization + pool increase), rounded to 4 decimals, share counts rounded down. Same as yc does it.

Exits

Prefs get paid by seniority, pro rata inside a rank. Whatever's left goes to common, exercised options, participating preferred (up to any cap) and any class that converted. Options only exercise if common is above the strike. Each class converts if that pays more, and the engine settles everyone's choice together. Unconverted safes take the better of their money back or converting at the cap.

What it skips

No anti-dilution, no cumulative dividends, no MFN side letters, no tax. Use it to get your head around a round before you sign, then get a lawyer to read the actual docs.
